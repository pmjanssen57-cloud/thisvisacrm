Agreement Studio help

Use the Agreement Studio to define the services, fees and terms for a client, intake applicant or standalone matter, then issue a secure agreement for online acceptance.

1. Client & matter

Confirm the source, recipient, application type, included people, adviser and email details. Intake-linked agreements remain connected when the intake is later converted to a client.

2. Scope and agreement wording

Use Content to include only the services being undertaken. Edit the section wording where the engagement needs a specific qualification, exclusion or additional service.

3. Professional fees

Use Add payment or Edit to open the full fee editor. Record a clear description of the work covered, the dollar amount and the event or date that makes the payment due.

4. Government fees

Record the Agency, Application type and current Dollar value. Government charges are time-sensitive, so confirm the figures immediately before issue.

5. Signing

Check each signatory’s legal name and email address. The secure link records the agreement version, declarations, typed name, signature, date and technical audit details.

6. Template governance

Administrators can maintain the reusable master agreement and publish numbered versions. An agreement already sent or accepted remains tied to the version issued.

7. Issue and PDF

Complete both workflow confirmations before issuing. The assigned adviser is copied into the email. Print / save PDF produces the clean agreement pages without the Studio controls.

Before you issue
  • Read the scope as the client will understand it.
  • Check every professional payment stage and trigger.
  • Confirm government fees and signatory email addresses.
  • Use a new version if terms change after the agreement has been sent.
Accepted agreements are locked. Supersede and reissue rather than altering an agreement the client has already accepted.
