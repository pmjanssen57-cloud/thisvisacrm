THiS CRM v0.17.1 - MATTER WORKSPACE GUIDE

MY WORK
Use My Work as the normal adviser starting point.
- Needs my attention: adviser-owned work and files lacking a next action.
- Waiting on client: client/third-party items that are not currently adviser work.
- Waiting on INZ: applications genuinely waiting for Immigration New Zealand.
- Ready to progress: files where the next substantive step can now be taken.

CLIENT MATTER
Opening a client takes you to the Matter Command Centre.
The page should answer four questions immediately:
1. What stage are we at?
2. Who/what are we waiting for?
3. What happens next?
4. Is anything overdue or missing?

UPDATE FILE
Use + Update file for meaningful file events rather than separately editing several CRM fields.
1. Choose what happened.
2. Review the suggested stage/status/action/review/portal housekeeping.
3. Adjust anything that is file-specific.
4. Confirm and save.

WAITING STATES
A matter marked Waiting on INZ or Waiting on third party should have a review date. The review date is what brings the file back into operational attention.

FULL RECORD
Use Full record when detailed client data, family information, documents, key dates, billing, portal configuration, notes/strategy or other existing controls are needed. Nothing has been removed; it has been moved behind the operational view.

PORTAL WORDING
Use the standard wording selector as a starting point. Advisers can edit both the Current update and What happens next fields where the standard language does not fit the individual file.

ENQUIRY ARCHIVE
The Archive under Enquiries & Intake retains form information while removing linked intake CV blobs from Netlify storage. Restoring a record does not recreate a purged CV.
