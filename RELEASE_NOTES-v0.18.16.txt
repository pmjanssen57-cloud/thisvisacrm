THiS CRM v0.18.16 - Residence Approved guides

Nine distinct Instructions Studio packs: SMC, Straight to Residence, Work to Residence, Partnership, Parent Resident, Parent Retirement, AIP Growth, AIP Balanced, and Other Resident Visa.

Each contains a warm congratulatory message, visa-specific ongoing conditions and PRV timetable, citizenship eligibility, travel alerts, practical next steps, verified Turner Hopkins Lawyers service links, and an optional Google review invitation.

Guides remain editable in Instructions Studio and are issued through existing document workflow. No schema migration. Existing packs and drafts unchanged.

Important: The Google Maps link opens a business search, not a verified direct review URL. Replace with verified review link if available. Visa conditions and historic policy cohorts must be checked against each approval before issue. Particularly: Parent PRV 10 years; Parent Retirement investment 4 years; AIP Growth 36 months and Balanced 60 months; citizenship requirements differ from PRV.
