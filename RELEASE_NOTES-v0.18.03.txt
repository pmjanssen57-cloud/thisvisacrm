THiS CRM v0.18.03 - Independent Agreement Signing & Partner Email
13 November 2026

BASELINE: v0.18.02 (Instructions Studio master checklist retained).

- Client record > Family details > Spouse/partner now has a persistent partner email field.
  It is saved within the existing family_members JSONB, so no SQL migration is needed.
- Agreement Studio seeds the partner's email from the client record, preserving deliberate adviser overrides.
- Partner and principal signatories retain separate recipient identities and signing URLs even when the same email inbox is used.
- Fixes agreement preview signature matching: person name/role takes priority over shared email addresses.
- Secure public agreement view retrieves the current non-superseded signature status and captured image for each person; the final display can show both signatures rather than just the signer opening the link.
- Signing confirmation now identifies the intended person and role.
- Agreement editor labels signatory fields and warns advisers when both people share an inbox.
- Prevents accidental reissue when any signature has already been accepted, without resetting the recorded agreement status.
- Superseded links can no longer be used for new acceptances.
- Email/signatory status and other CRM records retain their previous data model.

VERIFICATION:
- node --check passed for edited server functions and public agreement-studio.js.
- Signature matching regression checks passed for both signing orders with a shared inbox and partner-only signing.
- ZIP integrity and package contents checked.
- Complete npm/Vite build and live Netlify/Graph/database and browser end-to-end tests NOT completed here.

DEPLOYMENT:

- Verify signing second does not alter first's captured signature and that the printed agreement shows both.
- Verify partner email survives saving/reopening client Family details and automatically prefills new agreements.
- Do not rely on this build as a remediation for already corrupted historical signatures without inspecting original DB acceptance rows.
