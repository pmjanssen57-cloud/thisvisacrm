THiS CRM v0.17.38 - Incomplete Assessment Duplicate Visibility

The Incomplete Assessments screen now flags likely duplicate records using the applicant email address.

Possible duplicate: two or more active saved drafts use the same normalised email address.
Previous assessment on file: the same email appears on an already submitted full assessment in THiS, including archived intake history.

The screen shows the relevant counts and the most recent matching draft/submitted assessment information. These are advisory flags only. No record is automatically merged or deleted.

Database: unchanged at 46 migrations.
