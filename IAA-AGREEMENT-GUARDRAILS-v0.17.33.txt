THiS Agreement Studio - IAA written-agreement guardrails (v0.17.33)

This release adds software checks intended to help advisers meet the current IAA written-agreement requirements.

Before issue THiS requires:
- client / organisation
- named adviser and IAA licence number
- explicit licence type
- provisional supervisor details where applicable
- limited licence authorised matters where applicable
- tailored scope
- completed professional fee stages and payment milestones
- current government charges / reasonable estimates where listed
- likely third-party disbursements or a statement that none are presently identified
- conflict / benefit disclosure
- required signatory email(s)
- confirmations that fees/disbursements and client/scope details were checked
- confirmation adviser licence details were checked
- confirmation current IAA Professional Standards were provided and explained
- confirmation internal complaints procedure was provided
- confirmation significant terms were explained before acceptance

The agreement records written authority where representation is within scope. Issued emails contain official IAA Professional Standards and Code of Conduct links.

The adviser remains responsible for the underlying professional obligations and the accuracy of the matter-specific content.
