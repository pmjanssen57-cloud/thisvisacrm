THiS CRM v0.17.29 - Unified Client & Commercial Matter Workspace

NORMAL USER FLOW

Clients -> choose Individual or Commercial -> Open -> Update matter / Reschedule -> My Work

CLIENTS REGISTER
- All clients: individual and commercial together.
- Individuals: individual immigration clients only.
- Commercial: employer organisations only.
- Commercial records are identified by a Commercial badge and building icon.

COMMERCIAL MATTER WORKFLOW
Commercial clients now share the normal operational matter controls:
- primary adviser / backup adviser;
- Needs my attention;
- Waiting on client;
- Waiting on INZ;
- Ready to progress;
- next action;
- action/review date;
- priority;
- Update matter;
- Reschedule; and
- My Work / dashboard workload visibility.

COMMERCIAL-SPECIFIC TOOLS
Open a commercial client to access:
- Overview / organisation and accreditation;
- Workers;
- Job Checks;
- Compliance;
- Documents;
- Employer Portal; and
- Activity.

TECHNICAL DESIGN
Commercial clients remain in the existing commercial_clients table. Existing worker, Job Check, compliance, document, portal-user and audit relationships are preserved. The CRM presents them through the same matter workflow as individual clients rather than migrating commercial data into the individual clients table.

DATABASE
Migration 45 adds additive workflow columns to commercial_clients. Historical migrations are unchanged.
