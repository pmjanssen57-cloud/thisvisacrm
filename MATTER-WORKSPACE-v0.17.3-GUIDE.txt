THiS CRM v0.17.3 - MATTER WORKSPACE GUIDE

MY WORK DATE FILTER
My Work defaults to Today + overdue. Use the date buttons above the kanban to widen the view:
- Today + overdue: normal daily working view.
- Next 7 days: short-term planning.
- Next 30 days: broader forward planning.
- All scheduled: show the full active schedule.

Files with no next action, or waiting files with no review date, remain visible as safety exceptions.

HOW THE FOUR COLUMNS WORK
The columns are operating states, not sequential matter stages.
- Needs my attention: the adviser owns the next move.
- Waiting on client: the client or third party needs to provide/do something.
- Waiting on INZ: INZ owns the next move; the file returns on the review date or a new INZ event.
- Ready to progress: no blocker remains and the adviser can move the matter forward.

Typical examples:
- Client uploads a requested document -> Needs my attention.
- Application is lodged -> Waiting on INZ.
- Final checklist becomes complete -> Ready to progress.
- INZ issues a request -> Needs my attention.

MOVING A CARD
Open the matter and use + Update File. Changing the Matter status moves the card automatically. Do not treat the board as a drag/drop stage tracker: the Update File workflow also maintains the next action, review/due date, portal wording and timeline.

CLIENT MATTER
Opening a client takes you to the Matter Command Centre. The page should answer four questions immediately:
1. What stage are we at?
2. Who/what are we waiting for?
3. What happens next?
4. Is anything overdue or missing?

WAITING STATES
A waiting matter should have a review date. That review date is its primary My Work date and determines when it returns to the adviser workspace. Adviser-owned work instead uses the Next Action Due date.

FULL RECORD
Use Full record for detailed client data, family information, documents, key dates, billing, portal configuration, strategy/notes and other existing controls.
