THiS CRM v0.17.50 — quick guide

My Work: click anywhere on a matter row to open its working drawer. Review the snapshot,
then scroll to the existing update controls. Save changes records the update as before.
Use Open full client record when you need the wider file. Cancel a discard prompt to keep
unsaved edits in the drawer. Waiting rows and Board cards use this same drawer.

The empty Today view offers direct links to Next 7 Days and All Work. Existing date and
responsibility filters remain. View options switches List/Board; Shortcuts retains actions.

Matter journey: ticks indicate completed stages; the dark green node is current; pale
nodes are upcoming. On phones swipe the stage strip to see earlier/later stages. The current
stage also appears in the header. Stage editing and completion rules have not changed.

Enquiries: Contacts, Intake, Employer Forms, Seminars, Feedback, Incomplete, Archive.
Employer Forms retains the v0.17.49 assessment, save, PDF/download, attach/create workflow.

No new database migration is required when updating from v0.17.49.
