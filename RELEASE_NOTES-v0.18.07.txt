THiS CRM v0.18.07 - Per-section Required for visibility
17 November 2026

BASELINE: THiS CRM v0.18.06.

- In Instructions Studio, open a client Documentation Guide section and use the new "Show Required for in this section" checkbox.
- The choice is saved on that section in the client draft, without changing person assignments or any master template.
- It is on by default for ordinary evidence sections and off by default for general advice sections, including Helpful hints and final steps, Remember, Notes on documentation, and Questions/next steps.
- When off, the preview and printed document omit the per-request Required for line for that section. Existing applicant selections remain intact and return if switched back on.
- Hides the same line in a legacy non-people-based document preview when explicitly disabled.
- Existing checklist editing, section numbering, agreements and employer templates are unchanged.
