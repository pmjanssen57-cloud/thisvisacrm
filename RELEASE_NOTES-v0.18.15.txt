THiS CRM v0.18.15 - Administrative Agreement Status

- Agreement lists now provide Change status for non-electronically accepted agreements.
- Record partially signed, completed externally, cancelled, superseded and re-opened administrative statuses.
- Optional signer name and signing date; explanatory note required for closure and partial signing.
- Audit table tracks actor, timestamp, before and after statuses and notes.
- Electronically accepted agreements remain immutable; manual statuses do not fabricate electronic signatures.
- Public links are blocked once a record is closed administratively.
- Filed agreements are protected from deletion; no data removed.
- Additive database schema on boot: agreement_sets.manual_status and agreement_status_audit.
- Rollback: v0.18.14. Data fields are additive and retained after rollback.
