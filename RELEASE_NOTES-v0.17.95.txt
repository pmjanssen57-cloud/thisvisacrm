THiS CRM v0.17.95 — Instructions Studio Cards and Steps
12 November 2026

Changes
- In the master template and reusable block editor, select a card or a timeline step to reveal controls above the editor.
- Remove selected card / step, or remove the complete Cards / Steps group, without deleting adjacent wording.
- Updates are reflected in the live section preview and marked as unsaved edits.
- Cards use a tidy two-column grid, switching to one column at narrow widths.
- Step labels have a fixed-width, non-wrapping column to prevent labels breaking into fragments.
- Editor-only buttons are outside the contenteditable area, and are not saved in template HTML or included in client documents.
- Existing library storage structure and client document generation logic are unchanged.

- Version and cache-busting URLs updated to 0.17.95.
- Do not overwrite production databases or Netlify environment variables.
- Save master changes and explicitly Publish before they become the starting point for new documents.
