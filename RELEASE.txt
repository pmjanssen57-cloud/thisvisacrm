v0.14.3 - Closed Chat Cleanup and Client Lifecycle Controls

- Added a permanent Delete action for closed website-chat conversations.
- Closed chat deletion removes the conversation, messages, internal notes and chat audit events through the existing cascading database relationships.
- Deletion is restricted to closed chats and is available to the assigned adviser, an unassigned chat owner context, or an administrator.
- A linked Enquiries & Intake record is retained when its originating chat is deleted.
- Added an explicit Close client action under the client record More menu, with a matching Reopen client action.
- Closing a client retains the complete client record, timeline, dates, documents, billing history, portal data, instructions and agreements.
- Closed clients are excluded from dashboard/task generation, including next actions, client deadlines, document expiries, linked personal tasks, linked calendar appointments and dashboard billing signals.
- Reopening a client reactivates the saved dates and linked work without recreating them.
- Closed records remain searchable in the Clients workspace and are clearly marked with a Closed badge and status banner.
- Added contextual Help guidance for the client-closing workflow.
- Updated the service-worker and embedded Studio asset versions so the revised interface loads after deployment.
- No database migration, dependency change, additional software or subscription is required.
