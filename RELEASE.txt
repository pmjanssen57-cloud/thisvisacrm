THiS CRM v0.17.44 - Employer Accreditation Questionnaire

Changes
- Adds a public six-step Employer Accreditation Questionnaire at /employer-accreditation, designed for embedding on the Turner Hopkins website.
- Rebuilds the existing Logiforms employer accreditation questionnaire into the current THiS guided-form style, while updating the questions for the current Standard / High-volume / Triangular accreditation framework.
- Collects employer contact and business details, NZBN/company number, GST number, IRD number, entity type, business operation length, accreditation application/type, viability basis, workforce/employment practices, settlement support and current compliance declarations.
- Collects repeatable key-person details including full name, role, date of birth, gender, country of birth, citizenship, passport number, visa status and optional email.
- Adds conditional questions for partnerships/trusts/sole traders and triangular employment arrangements.
- Supports secure 30-day draft/resume, Save & finish later, autosave after valid contact details, and retained supporting-document uploads.
- Supporting documents submitted with the questionnaire are copied into the linked commercial client's Documents register.
- Submitted questionnaires match an existing commercial client by NZBN first, then contact email + legal name; otherwise a commercial client is created for adviser review.
- Commercial client records now store GST/IRD numbers and show an Accreditation questionnaire summary/history with declarations, key people, uploads and duplicate indicators.
- Adds confirmation email to the employer and internal/adviser notification email, both logged in the existing email notification register.
- Adds duplicate checks by contact email and NZBN.
- Emits the public conversion bridge event employer_accreditation for the Squarespace/GA4 listener.
- Includes turner-hopkins-employer-accreditation-embed.html as the Squarespace iframe helper.
- Adds migration 47 only; the previous 46 migrations are unchanged.
