THiS CRM v0.15.9 - Agreement Acceptance Visibility Hotfix

Baseline
- Built directly from v0.15.7 Live Chat Contact & Intake Handoff.
- This hotfix intentionally does not include the v0.15.8 Unified Client Record Editor, so the agreement correction can be deployed and verified first.

Agreement acceptance
- Agreement Studio now refreshes the agreement record from the server whenever an adviser opens it.
- The Agreements workspace refreshes agreement statuses when the workspace is opened, so newly accepted agreements are reflected in the list.
- Accepted and partially signed agreements display a CRM acceptance summary with each signatory's current status.
- The signed agreement now overlays the secure acceptance record onto the saved Studio document rather than relying on the pre-issue Studio snapshot.
- The Authorisation and acceptance section displays the captured electronic signature, typed legal name, signatory email, and New Zealand acceptance date/time.
- The Signing panel shows Sent, Viewed, and Accepted states and confirms when an electronic signature has been recorded.
- Accepted agreements are locked against subsequent Studio saves on the server. Print / save PDF remains available.
- The secure client review page also reflects the accepted signatory's recorded signature details after acceptance.
- Agreement Studio cache-busting and the PWA shell cache have been advanced so the corrected Studio script is not masked by an older cached copy.

No database migration, dependency or environment-variable changes.
