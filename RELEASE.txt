THiS CRM v0.17.3 - My Work Focus and Board Guidance

Baseline
- Built directly from v0.17.2 Visual Polish and Standard Menus.
- Retains the complete Matter Workspace, configurable notification recipients, assessment-form reliability, enquiry archiving, Agreement Studio print fixes and all existing CRM functionality.

My Work date focus
- My Work now defaults to Today + overdue rather than showing every future review/action date at once.
- Adds explicit date-range controls: Today + overdue, Next 7 days, Next 30 days, and All scheduled.
- Safety matters with no next action or missing waiting review dates remain visible regardless of the selected range.
- The Suggested next file action now respects the selected date range.
- The My Work sidebar badge now reflects work requiring attention now rather than counting all future adviser-owned work.

Clearer board behaviour
- Adds a visible How cards move guide above the kanban.
- Clarifies that the four columns are operating states, not sequential immigration stages.
- Explains what each state means: adviser action, client/third-party action, waiting on INZ, and ready to progress.
- Adds real examples of common state changes such as document receipt, lodgement and checklist completion.
- Adds concise descriptions beneath each column heading.
- Kanban cards now include a clearer Open matter cue.
- Cards continue to move automatically when Matter status is changed through Update File; no separate drag/drop state is introduced, preserving workflow integrity.

Work-date behaviour
- Adviser/action matters use Next Action Due as their primary work date.
- Waiting matters use Matter Review Date as their primary return-to-work date.
- This avoids stale review dates masking a live adviser action date.

Database and deployment
- No database migration is added.
- All 43 v0.17.2 migrations remain unchanged.
- No new dependency or environment variable is required.
- Package/cache references advance to v0.17.3.
