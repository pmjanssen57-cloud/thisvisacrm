THiS CRM v0.17.35 - Compact Mobile Status Cards

- Tightens the phone layout introduced in v0.17.34.
- My Work status metrics now fit as a compact 3 x 2 grid instead of oversized/swipe-heavy cards.
- Dashboard/task/calendar/billing metric cards remain in a compact two-column layout on phones.
- Removes the <=480px rule that forced metric cards into one large full-width column.
- Reduces mobile work-state column, matter-card, status-pill and action-control spacing while retaining touch targets.
- Keeps 16px form controls and the no-zoom mobile input behaviour from v0.17.34.
- Desktop layout and CRM workflow are unchanged.

Database: unchanged at 45 migrations.
