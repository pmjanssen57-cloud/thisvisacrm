THiS CRM v0.17.50 — My Work & Matter Journey polish
20 October 2026. Baseline: confirmed THiS CRM v0.17.49.

MY WORK
- Every matter row opens the same right-side Update matter drawer, including identity,
  next-action, date and arrow areas, Today, Next 7 days, All work, waiting/expanded waiting
  and Board cards. Row activation also works by keyboard.
- Working snapshot includes the client/matter heading, stage, status, next action/date,
  responsibility, main/backup adviser and a recorded key date where available.
- Open full client record is an explicit separate action. Unsaved edits require a discard
  decision before navigating; opening records makes no data changes.
- Existing quick update/save, failed-save recovery, portal updates and stage logic remain.
- Waiting for others has explanatory guidance; existing responsibility tags remain.
- Today empty state: soft mint treatment, check icon, You're up to date / No actions are
  due today, with View Next 7 Days and View All Work actions.
- More prominent timing and weekday/date details in Next 7 days.
- View options and Shortcuts use compact bordered controls with clear disclosure icons.

MATTER JOURNEY
- Ticked completed stages, prominent current stage, readable labels and clear connectors.
- Current-stage summary replaces the explanatory prose. Compact desktop stage strip.
- On smaller screens the strip scrolls within its own area; the current stage is revealed
  on opening and viewport resizing. Full-page horizontal overflow is avoided.
- Stage selection, definitions, completion rules and saved data are unchanged.

ENQUIRIES & INTAKE
Contacts -> Intake -> Employer Forms -> Seminars -> Feedback -> Incomplete -> Archive.
Consistent tab heights, padding, icon sizing, visible count badges and selected treatment.
The label change does not alter employer questionnaire records, filters or handling.
