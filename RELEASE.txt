THiS CRM v0.15.6 - Finished Master Road Maps

Instructions Studio
- Rebuilt the Introduction & Road Map model around the existing THiS/Panda approach: a finished standard document that the adviser tweaks rather than a blank drafting framework.
- Applied the model across all instruction packs.
- Restored a full SMC Road Map structure: Introduction, Your Strategy, Category Requirements, Steps & Timelines, Skilled Employment, and Questions & Next Steps.
- Removed the Application at a glance page from Road Maps.
- Removed mandatory five-part adviser-authored Road Map fields and their pre-issue blocking checks.
- Road Map substantive content no longer depends on CRM assessment conclusions or detailed client fields.
- Added simple section include/reorder controls and step include/exclude controls, kept behind a collapsed advanced section.
- Direct preview editing remains the normal way to personalise wording, headings and timeframes.
- Published Road Map master edits in the Template Library are preserved across CRM reloads and are used for future client drafts.
- Packs that previously combined Road Map and evidence content now expose separate Road Map and Documentation Guide documents while preserving the existing evidence blocks.
- Documentation Guide, police-clearance selection, specialist guides, template governance and PDF/portal workflows remain in place.

No database migration, dependency or environment-variable changes.
