v0.13.57 - Integrated Agreement Studio

Built from the authoritative v0.13.56 Integrated Instructions Studio package.

Adds:
- Agreement Studio alongside Instructions in the main CRM navigation.
- Client-linked and standalone engagement agreements.
- Reusable application-specific scopes, professional fee schedules and Government fee schedules.
- Database-backed agreement drafts, master templates and immutable template versions.
- Secure tokenised client review and electronic acceptance with declarations, typed name, drawn signature and audit details.
- Microsoft 365 issue email to each required signatory, with the assigned adviser copied and used as reply-to.
- Agreement status workflow: Draft, Ready, Sent, Viewed, Partially signed, Accepted, Declined, Superseded, Cancelled and Archived.
- Accepted agreements are locked against later editing.
- Client record Agreements section and mobile navigation access.
- Code of Conduct link included in the client-facing agreement and acceptance declaration.

Database migration:
- 202608040002_add_agreement_studio.sql

Existing v0.13.56 Instructions Studio and all v0.13.55 intake refresh, CV request and print-view functionality are retained.
