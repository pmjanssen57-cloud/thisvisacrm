THiS CRM v0.17.29 - Unified Client & Commercial Matter Workspace

Purpose
- Manage individual and commercial clients through one operational Clients / My Work experience without dismantling the specialist employer data model.

What changed
- Clients now combines individual and commercial records in one register.
- Commercial records have a Commercial badge and building icon.
- Client type filters provide All clients, Individuals and Commercial views.
- New individual and New commercial actions sit together in the Clients workspace.
- Commercial clients now use the standard matter workflow fields and My Work states.
- Commercial matters support Update matter and Reschedule.
- Commercial matter activity is recorded alongside the existing commercial audit trail.
- Commercial records now appear in My Work, Dashboard/client workload and dated next-action task views.
- Adviser workload counts include commercial matters when they are in the operational client view.
- The separate Commercial destination has been removed from normal desktop/mobile navigation.
- Employer-specific Workers, Job Checks, Compliance, Documents and Portal tools remain inside the commercial client record.
- Newly created commercial records are safely reconciled from their temporary browser ID to the saved database ID.
- Unsaved commercial records must be saved before operational Update matter / Reschedule actions are enabled.
- Clients and Commercial help text has been updated for the unified model.
- The PWA shell cache advances to v0.17.29.

Database
- One new additive migration: 202609190001_unify_commercial_matter_workflow.sql.
- Migration count increases from 44 to 45.
- The prior 44 migrations are unchanged.
- Existing commercial tables and relationships are preserved.

Rollback
- Use the supplied v0.17.29-to-v0.17.28 application rollback package.
- Migration 45 must remain present once applied; the rollback package includes it unchanged.
- The v0.17.28 application safely ignores the additive workflow columns.
