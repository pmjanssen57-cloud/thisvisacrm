THiS CRM v0.15.7 - Live Chat Contact & Intake Handoff

Live chat
- Replaced the old "Create enquiry" action that immediately created a full intake-style record.
- Added a single "Send intake form" action in the adviser chat window.
- The action creates a short Contact form record from the live chat, assigns it to the adviser responsible for the conversation, and retains the public chat transcript for reference.
- The Contact form is classified as a contact enquiry rather than a completed assessment questionnaire.
- The existing Contact assessment invitation email/template is then used to send the full assessment questionnaire link to the visitor.
- When the email is sent successfully, the Contact form is automatically marked Dealt with (Contacted).
- A private chat note records that the assessment questionnaire was emailed.
- If email delivery fails, the Contact form remains available in Enquiries > Contact forms so the adviser can retry the existing Send intake action there.
- Existing chats already linked to an enquiry/intake remain intact.

No database migration, dependency or environment-variable changes.
