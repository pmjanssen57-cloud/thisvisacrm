THiS CRM v0.17.10 - Streamlined Client Update

- Adds explicit Back to clients buttons to the Matter Command Centre and Full Record workspace.
- Adds a compact Update Client drawer to the Matter Command Centre.
- Separates Update Client (profile changes) from Update File (substantive matter workflow).
- Update Client categories: Matter & adviser, Contact, Personal, Family, Other.
- Matter & adviser includes stage, matter status, next action and due/review date as well as ownership/case type/priority.
- Simplifies Full Record Overview into read-only profile summaries with targeted Edit buttons.
- Retains the comprehensive full profile editor as a secondary fallback.
- Preserves client-list search/filter/adviser state when returning to Clients.
- No database migration, dependency or environment-variable change.
