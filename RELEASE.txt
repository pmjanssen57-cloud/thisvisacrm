THiS CRM v0.17.21 - Assessment Friction Reduction

Baseline
- Built from v0.17.20 Adviser Cleanup + Faster Assessment Flow.
- Retains draft/resume, CV uploads, second citizenship, full final review, adviser cleanup and all CRM functionality.

Assessment simplification
- Removes the technical interstitial/pop-up wording from the repeated stage reminder.
- The reminder now simply explains that progress saves securely and Save & finish later is available.
- Removes the redundant longer-term-goal question when the applicant has already selected Live in New Zealand permanently. It remains for Work in New Zealand and Not sure yet, where it adds useful context.
- Clarifies partner status wording: Partner current New Zealand visa (if any) and Is your partner a New Zealand citizen or resident?
- Keeps partner identity/relationship questions visible, but collapses detailed partner work and qualification fields behind an optional control.
- Partner CV upload is presented before the optional detailed work/qualification fields, with clear wording that uploading a CV normally means those details can be left blank.
- Reworks the save-progress explanation so it stays together as one readable block instead of splitting around the Save & finish later wording.
- Improves mobile width treatment for Back / Save & finish later controls.

Consent behaviour
- Consent attention styling is only triggered by a deliberate submission attempt.
- Consent warnings are cleared when the applicant leaves the final review step to edit earlier answers.

Squarespace promotional pop-up
- The SMC promotional pop-up is controlled by Squarespace, outside the Netlify iframe. It cannot be disabled safely from the CRM build.
- Exclude /assessment under Squarespace Promotional pop-up -> Display & timing -> Pages, and update the old 24 August 2026 promotional wording where the pop-up continues to run.

Database
- No new migration.
- All 44 existing migrations remain unchanged.
