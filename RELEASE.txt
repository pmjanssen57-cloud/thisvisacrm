THiS CRM v0.17.79 — 9 November 2026

APPLICATION-SPECIFIC AGREEMENT SERVICES
Based on v0.17.78. New agreements now seed relevant services and professional payment rows for SMC, Partnership, Parent Residence, Active Investor Plus, AEWV, AEWV family, Employer Accreditation, Student, Visitor, Permanent Residence, Citizenship and Other. There is no fixed four-service requirement. The original shared SMC fee schedule no longer seeds unrelated agreements.

Add service opens a searchable library of relevant defaults and optional IQA, registration, additional temporary/family applications, partnership residence, Parent EOI, Job Check, accreditation renewal, investor compliance and additional issue assessment. Add custom service creates editable scope and a linked payment. Advisers can edit, reorder, duplicate and remove payments. Duplication copies linked scope independently and clears the amount for review. Removal offers payment-only removal for bundling, or removal of the service and payment together; scope shared by another payment is retained.

The payment editor lets several services be linked to a single payment. Current linked scope wording appears in the fee editor and the agreement/PDF table. Updating scope wording therefore updates the linked work in the fee schedule. Removed/excluded links and unpriced service coverage are highlighted and block issue until resolved. Existing unrelated legacy fee rows are not forcibly linked or rewritten.

New professional fees start blank. New/reloaded government cost rows say Confirm current, so amounts and payment milestones must be reviewed before issue. Optional work is deliberately added: SMC does not automatically include IQA, registration or temporary/family visas; employer accreditation does not include Job Checks or renewals; investor compliance is an optional addition. Partnership defaults cover a selected temporary visa; residence is separately available. Parent EOI is optional. Additional government cost rows are entered separately when relevant work is added.

DRAFT AND SIGNED AGREEMENT PRESERVATION
Saved drafts retain their scope, payment descriptions, amounts, triggers and government costs. Nothing is automatically converted on opening. Load suggested services is an explicit, CRM-styled confirmation that replaces those rows and resets amounts. Changing application type offers Cancel, Keep my services or Load suggested services. Keep retains the commercial rows and prompts review of the matter description. Suggested-service changes are blocked for Sent, Viewed, Partially signed and Accepted snapshots. Core terms, signatures, acceptance, issuing and backend routes are unchanged. New profile metadata and scope links persist in the existing studioState JSON; no new table or column is required.

LAYOUT AND OUTPUT
Searchable library and confirmations share the existing green/mint Studio styling, with keyboard focus, native dialog focus containment/Escape, wrapping controls and small-screen scrolling. Payment editor actions remain reachable on mobile. Existing live preview and print pagination are retained. Service wording is escaped text in preview/output. agreement-services.js is included before the main engine and in PWA precache; main agreement script cache query and shell version advance to v0.17.79.
