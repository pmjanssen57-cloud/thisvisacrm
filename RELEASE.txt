- Adds both Studio scripts to the service-worker pre-cache and advances the shell cache to v0.13.57.4.
- Restores Agreement Type options, template data, live A4 previews and CRM payload initialisation in deployed builds.
- Corrects source-specific loading messages for linked-client, intake-linked and standalone documents.
- Adds agreement creation from unconverted intake forms, with search by name, email, pathway or status.

- Automatically links intake-originated agreements to the new client record when that intake is converted, while retaining intake provenance.
