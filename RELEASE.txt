THiS CRM v0.17.14 — Simple Matter Update + Advanced Editing

Workflow direction
- Day-to-day adviser work is now centred on one Update matter action.
- The normal update captures what happened, what happens next, the next/review date and who has the ball.
- Portal publishing is optional inside the same update and includes editable preset wording.
- A separate Reschedule shortcut changes only the next/review date.
- Complete / Change / Update File are removed from the normal matter screen to reduce competing actions.

Advanced editing
- Documents, Billing, Key dates and Stages are grouped under a clear Matter tools panel.
- Each tool opens the existing detailed client record directly at the relevant section.
- Client details remain separate from matter progression.
- Advanced record remains available for full control without exposing that complexity in the everyday workflow.

Mobile
- The matter screen uses a compact sticky bottom action bar for Update matter and Reschedule.
- Update matter becomes a mobile bottom sheet with a sticky save area and single-column fields.
- Secondary actions remain under More / Client details rather than consuming the mobile header.

Database
- No new migration. All existing migrations remain unchanged.
