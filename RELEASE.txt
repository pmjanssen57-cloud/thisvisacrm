THiS CRM v0.17.20 - Agreement Email Routing Reliability

Agreement Studio
- Intake/client hydration no longer overwrites adviser name/email or recipient details that have been deliberately edited in the Agreement Studio.
- The principal client email and principal signatory email are kept in sync while editing.
- Editing a principal signatory name/email also updates the Client & matter principal details.
- Other required signatory email edits remain authoritative for issue delivery.
- Issue Agreement now previews the actual current required-signatory To addresses and the current adviser CC address.
- Each required signatory continues to receive an individual secure signing link.
- The Microsoft 365 email log records the same live To/CC routing used for the send.
- Acceptance confirmation emails continue to use the adviser email stored in the issued Agreement Studio snapshot.

Database
- No database migration.
- Existing 44 migrations are unchanged.
