THiS CRM v0.17.86 — 10 November 2026

SEMINAR BULK ACTIONS
In Enquiries > Seminars, choose one event under Registrations for. Remind approved opens a recipient and message preview. Send now queues an immediate background send; Schedule before the event offers 1 hour, 2 hours, 24 hours, 48 hours or one week. Edit the subject and extra message. Reminders contain the saved event title, date/time/timezone, presenter, joining link and password, plus the registrant's local time where available. Only approved registrations qualify. Each distinct valid email receives one private email per batch; duplicate registration records remain visible.

Scheduled batches collect current approvals when sending begins, including later approvals. The cohort is then frozen; eligibility is rechecked before each send. Pending reminders can be cancelled. Changing saved event details stops an existing reminder for review rather than sending an outdated link. Cancel that batch and create a new reminder after reviewing the new details. Sent/remaining/failed/uncertain/skipped counts appear in reminder history. Retry failed retries definite failures only. Uncertain sends require checking Microsoft Sent Items and the CRM email log; they are never automatically resent. Microsoft acceptance is recorded as Sent; it is not proof of inbox delivery.

Archive approved closes public registration for the selected seminar, stops its pending reminders and archives its approved entries only. New, declined, spam and other seminars remain unchanged. See Seminars > Archived and Restore to approved to recover individual entries without sending an approval email. Restoring an entry does not reopen a closed seminar. An email already in progress can finish after cancellation/archive.

SCHEDULING / CONFIGURATION
Uses the existing Microsoft mail configuration and Netlify URL; no new secret is needed. The Netlify scheduler runs every five minutes on the production deployment, with signed background dispatch. Send now requests dispatch immediately; a failed dispatch leaves the job queued for the next tick. Work is processed in chunks of up to 20 recipients per tick, so larger queues may take multiple ticks. Schedule with sufficient lead time. No reminder is sent after the event starts. Optional SEMINAR_REMINDERS_ENABLED=false disables sending and creation/retry while leaving history, cancellation and archiving available. Existing daily overdue emails are unchanged.

Apply the included migration through the normal database process if using migration-driven provisioning. No Squarespace embed changes are required.
