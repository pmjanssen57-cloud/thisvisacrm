THiS CRM v0.17.19 - Assessment Form Review & Validation Polish

Assessment form improvements
- Clears the missing-name validation message as soon as both first and last names are entered.
- Adds a second-citizenship question to the applicant details step.
- The final Review & Send step now shows the full questionnaire in grouped expandable sections, with direct Edit links back to the relevant stage.
- Applicant and partner CV status is included in the final review where a file is saved/selected.
- Submitted CRM questionnaire review also displays second-citizenship answers.

Promotional pop-up
- The promotional pop-up reported during testing is a Squarespace site-level feature, not part of the CRM intake form. Exclude the Assessment page under Squarespace Promotional pop-up > Display & timing > Pages.

Database and deployment
- No new database migration.
- All existing 44 migration files are unchanged from v0.17.18.
- Existing v0.17.17+ Squarespace resume-token embed remains compatible and does not need replacing for this release.
