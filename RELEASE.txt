THiS CRM v0.15.5 - Adviser-led Instructions Authoring

- Rebuilds the SMC Road Map around five simple adviser-authored client sections.
- Limits CRM population to administrative details rather than automatically generating substantive advice.
- Adds guided drafting prompts and optional, deliberately inserted starter snippets.
- Makes Documentation Guide sections adviser-selected and disabled by default.
- Requires client-specific guidance for every selected evidence section.
- Includes deliberately selected police-clearance country instructions.
- Adds a pre-issue completeness check and explicit responsible-adviser review confirmation.
- Blocks print and issue while critical content is incomplete or contains placeholders.
- Simplifies client-facing evidence sections and removes repetitive technical card clutter.
- Corrects SMC print flow to prevent blank pages, isolated headings and clipped sections.
- No database migration, API, dependency or environment-variable changes.
