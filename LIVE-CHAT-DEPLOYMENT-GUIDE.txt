THiS CRM v0.15.16 - LIVE CHAT DEPLOYMENT GUIDE

1. Deploy the complete v0.15.16 package to the existing Netlify site.

2. Confirm the existing LIVE_CHAT_SESSION_SECRET remains configured for Functions and marked as containing secret values.

3. Confirm the existing Microsoft Graph email variables remain configured:
   MICROSOFT_TENANT_ID
   MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID
   MICROSOFT_CLIENT_SECRET
   MICROSOFT_NOTIFICATION_FROM_EMAIL
   MICROSOFT_NOTIFICATION_FROM_NAME

4. Optional: LIVE_CHAT_NOTIFICATION_RECIPIENTS can remain configured as a comma-separated fallback.

5. Open the deployed CRM and perform a hard refresh.

6. Open Tools > Live chat settings and confirm:
   - chat enabled;
   - the intended weekly opening hours;
   - away dates;
   - the privacy notice URL; and
   - email notification recipients.

7. The existing Squarespace embed remains valid. The current generated version is:
   <script src="https://thisvisacrm.netlify.app/live-chat-widget.js?v=0.15.16" data-title="Chat with us" defer></script>

8. Test during open hours:
   - Start a website chat.
   - Confirm the one-time email notification arrives.
   - Confirm the CRM badge updates within about 10 seconds.
   - Claim the chat and exchange messages.

9. Test outside hours with no open conversations:
   - Close all test chats.
   - Set an away date or test after the configured closing time.
   - Confirm the CRM remains functional and the chat badge refreshes immediately when the browser regains focus.
   - Start an after-hours website message and confirm the one-time email notification arrives.
   - Return focus to the CRM and confirm the message appears and regular polling resumes while it remains waiting.

The widget does not support attachments and must not be used for confidential document exchange.

10. Test adviser quick replies:
   - Claim a website chat.
   - Open Quick replies above the Reply composer.
   - Select the welcome, assessment, email and telephone replies and confirm the visitor/adviser fields are populated.
   - Confirm the selected reply is inserted for review and is not sent until the adviser presses Send.

11. Administrators can maintain the shared reply library under Tools > Live chat settings > Adviser quick replies. Keep replies concise and do not use them to give case-specific immigration advice without reviewing the visitor's circumstances.

CHAT TO CONTACT / ASSESSMENT HANDOFF

12. In an adviser conversation, use Send intake form when the visitor should complete the full assessment questionnaire.
13. The CRM creates an assigned Contact form from the visitor details and chat transcript, then sends the existing assessment invitation email/link.
14. On a successful send the Contact form is marked Dealt with. If delivery fails, open Enquiries > Contact forms and retry Send intake from the retained New contact.
15. Do not use live chat as a substitute for the full assessment questionnaire where detailed eligibility information is required.

DATABASE-IDLE / RUNTIME CHECKS

16. v0.15.16 retains the Netlify Blob-backed live-chat settings, attention and conversation-revision markers introduced in v0.15.14. No new environment variable is required.
17. With the website chat panel never opened, confirm the visitor iframe is not loaded. The visible launcher may still check open/closed status every five minutes while the page is visible; after cache warm-up this is served from Blob state rather than Postgres.
18. Hide the website tab and confirm launcher/message polling stops until the tab becomes visible again.
19. Hide the CRM tab and confirm staff chat polling stops until visibility returns. Signed-out CRM sessions must not run staff polling.
20. During an active conversation, confirm only messages newer than the browser's last message are returned after the initial load.
21. Outside configured opening hours, close all waiting/active chats and confirm the CRM sleeps routine chat checks until the next opening time (apart from focus/online wake events).
22. Keep the database provider's inactivity sleep at five minutes. If the provider plan offers a maximum-compute control, test a one- or two-unit cap separately; this is an account-side database setting rather than a CRM deployment setting.
