THiS CRM v0.15.3 - LIVE CHAT DEPLOYMENT GUIDE

1. Deploy the complete v0.15.3 package to the existing Netlify site.

2. Confirm the existing LIVE_CHAT_SESSION_SECRET remains configured for Functions and marked as containing secret values.

3. Confirm the existing Microsoft Graph email variables remain configured:
   MICROSOFT_TENANT_ID
   MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID
   MICROSOFT_CLIENT_SECRET
   MICROSOFT_NOTIFICATION_FROM_EMAIL
   MICROSOFT_NOTIFICATION_FROM_NAME

4. Optional: LIVE_CHAT_NOTIFICATION_RECIPIENTS can remain configured as a comma-separated fallback.

5. Open the deployed CRM and perform a hard refresh.

6. Open Tools > Live chat settings and confirm:
   - chat enabled;
   - the intended weekly opening hours;
   - away dates;
   - the privacy notice URL; and
   - email notification recipients.

7. The existing Squarespace embed remains valid. The current generated version is:
   <script src="https://thisvisacrm.netlify.app/live-chat-widget.js?v=0.15.3" data-title="Chat with us" defer></script>

8. Test during open hours:
   - Start a website chat.
   - Confirm the one-time email notification arrives.
   - Confirm the CRM badge updates within about 10 seconds.
   - Claim the chat and exchange messages.

9. Test outside hours with no open conversations:
   - Close all test chats.
   - Set an away date or test after the configured closing time.
   - Confirm the CRM remains functional and the chat badge refreshes immediately when the browser regains focus.
   - Start an after-hours website message and confirm the one-time email notification arrives.
   - Return focus to the CRM and confirm the message appears and regular polling resumes while it remains waiting.

The widget does not support attachments and must not be used for confidential document exchange.

10. Test adviser quick replies:
   - Claim a website chat.
   - Open Quick replies above the Reply composer.
   - Select the welcome, assessment, email and telephone replies and confirm the visitor/adviser fields are populated.
   - Confirm the selected reply is inserted for review and is not sent until the adviser presses Send.

11. Administrators can maintain the shared reply library under Tools > Live chat settings > Adviser quick replies. Keep replies concise and do not use them to give case-specific immigration advice without reviewing the visitor's circumstances.
