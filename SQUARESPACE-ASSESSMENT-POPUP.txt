Squarespace promotional pop-up

The promotional pop-up is controlled by Squarespace and sits outside the Netlify assessment iframe.

To stop it interrupting the assessment:
1. In Squarespace, open Promotional pop-up.
2. Open Display & timing.
3. Under Pages, choose Only certain pages.
4. Select the pages where the pop-up SHOULD appear and leave the Assessment page out.
5. Save.

No change to the v0.17.17 resume-aware assessment embed code is required for THiS CRM v0.17.19.
