Squarespace promotional pop-up - Assessment page

The SMC promotional pop-up is controlled by Squarespace and sits outside the Netlify assessment iframe, so a CRM deployment cannot reliably disable it.

In Squarespace:
1. Open Promotional pop-up.
2. Open Display & timing.
3. Under Pages, choose Only certain pages.
4. Select the pages where the pop-up SHOULD appear and leave /assessment out.
5. Save.
6. Update the promotional wording on the remaining pages so it no longer describes 24 August 2026 as a future date.

The resume-aware assessment embed does not need to change for v0.17.21.
