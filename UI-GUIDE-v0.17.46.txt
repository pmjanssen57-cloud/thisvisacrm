THiS CRM v0.17.46 — Quick guide

MY WORK
Start with Today & overdue; use Next 7 days or All work to widen the list.
Use the adviser selector and Responsibility filter to distinguish main/backup work.
Click the client name to open its matter, or the next action/pencil to update in a side panel.
Record what happened, the next responsibility/action and its due or review date.
Save changes keeps your place in My Work. Board is available if preferred.
Appointments, personal tasks, day overview and saved shortcuts are below the list.

CLIENTS
Search from the app header. Filter people/employers, status, adviser and matter type in Clients.
Use Client details for contact/personal/family edits, and Update matter for progress updates.
Open Documents, Stages, Dates, Billing or Portal, then choose its Edit action.
Make changes directly in that section and save to return to the summary.
For document checklists, select required evidence and mark a batch obtained/outstanding.

ENQUIRIES
Choose Contacts, Intake, Employer questionnaires or another submission type.
Open a row to review in the side panel. Intake triage is first; expand communication actions
or full answers when needed. Save & next works through the visible assessment queue.
Assign an adviser before individual client conversion. Employer submissions require an
explicit attach/create action after checking possible NZBN/name/email matches.
Processed history and duplicates remain available. Drafts are separate from submissions.

DOCUMENTS & TEMPLATES
Choose Agreements or Instructions. Use the New menu to select the available source.
Confirm the client/intake/standalone details and create the document.
Use Edit, Split view or Preview; on a phone use Sections to choose another section.
Agreement wording supports ordinary formatted text editing. Fees and signatories remain
structured fields. Next step moves through the authoring tabs.
Save to CRM saves a draft. Review the preview before issuing or marking ready.
Accepted agreements remain locked; the original issue/acceptance checks still apply.
Close Studio to return to the document list. Template library/data export are under More.

OTHER WORK
Calendar, Billing and the practice overview share the same visual language.
More keeps personal tasks, bookings, resources and administration accessible.
Settings & preferences opens personal workspace settings.
The header Tools button contains supporting tools and page Help.
