THiS CRM v0.17.37 - Incomplete Assessment Resume & Reminder Reliability

- Fixes the CRM resume-link action error: ensureEmailNotificationSchema is now defined in crm.mjs.
- Adds a separate one-time incomplete-assessment reminder email.
- Reminder email creates a fresh secure continuation token and refreshes the 30-day link expiry.
- Reminder sent date and sender are stored on the intake_drafts record and shown in CRM.
- The reminder button is disabled after a successful send.
- A partial unique email-notification index prevents concurrent duplicate reminder sends.
- Failed reminder sends remain retryable and restore the previously issued resume token where possible.
- Staff-sent resume/reminder emails no longer overwrite the applicant's Last active timestamp.
